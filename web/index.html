TamaPoke

A gentle Pokémon life-sim pet for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75: all 1025 species, English and 简体中文, weather, a farm and an album. Install it from your browser, with no Arduino and no drivers.

运行在 微雪 ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75 圆屏开发板上的宝可梦养成小宠物： 全部 1025 种宝可梦，中英文界面，有天气、农场和相册。在浏览器里就能安装，不用装 Arduino，也不用装驱动。

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. 这个浏览器不支持连接 USB 设备。请在电脑上用 Chrome 或 Edge 打开本页。

1Flash the firmware刷入固件

Plug the board into the computer with a USB-C cable, press the button and pick the board's port. It takes about a minute.

用 USB-C 数据线把开发板接到电脑，点下面的按钮，选择开发板的端口。大约需要一分钟。

Browser not supported (use Chrome or Edge).浏览器不支持（请用 Chrome 或 Edge）。 This page must be opened over HTTPS.本页需要通过 HTTPS 打开。

Updating? Leave "Erase device" unticked. The installer only writes the program, so your pet, points and Wi-Fi stay. Ticking it wipes the board, and your pet with it. Tick it only on a new board or one that ran other firmware.

升级时不要勾选 "Erase device"（擦除设备）。安装只写入程序，宠物、积分和 Wi-Fi 设置都会保留；勾选会清空整块板子，宠物也会没了。只有新板子或之前刷过别的固件时才勾选。

No port shows up? Try another cable (some only charge), or hold BOOT while plugging in.

找不到端口？换一根数据线试试（有的线只能充电），或者按住 BOOT 键再插上 USB。

2Prepare the microSD card准备 microSD 卡

The pictures (sprites, shiny sprites, Pokédex thumbnails and region landscapes) live on a FAT32 microSD card.

宝可梦图片（普通、异色、图鉴小图和地区风景）放在一张 FAT32 格式的 microSD 卡里。

Download the SD files下载 SD 卡文件 tamapoke-sd.zip

  1. Unzip it and copy the mons folder to the root of the card (files end up in /mons/).
  2. Put the card in the board and restart it.
  1. 解压后，把 mons 文件夹复制到卡的根目录（文件路径是 /mons/）。
  2. 把卡插进开发板，重启。

If the board can't read the card, it formats it, so back up anything on it first.

如果板子读不了这张卡，会自动格式化它，卡里原有的东西请先备份。

3Connect to Wi-Fi连接 Wi-Fi

Swipe down to the settings page and tap Wi-Fi. On your phone, join the network TamaPoke and open 192.168.4.1 to enter your Wi-Fi. The board uses it for the clock and the weather.

下滑打开设置页，点 Wi-Fi。用手机连接名为 TamaPoke 的网络，打开 192.168.4.1，填入你家的 Wi-Fi。板子会用它校准时间和获取天气。

Good to know

需要知道的

This build uses neutral settings: the clock and weather follow New York, and chess.com points are off. To use your own city, time zone and chess.com account, build it yourself with a config_private.h (see the README).

这个版本用的是通用设置：时间和天气按美国纽约，没有 chess.com 下棋积分。想用自己的城市、时区和 chess.com 账号，需要自己编译，填好 config_private.h（见中文说明）。

How to play, gestures and everything else: the repository.

玩法、手势和其他说明见仓库。
